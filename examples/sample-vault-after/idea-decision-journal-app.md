# Idea: Decision Journal App

app idea: a "decision journal" where you log decisions, your reasoning, and confidence level. then it reminds you to review them 30/90/180 days later to see if your reasoning was right

would help with calibrating your own judgment over time

could integrate with a simple bayesian model to show you your prediction accuracy by category
