# The Goldfinch — Donna Tartt

started reading jan 12

the opening at the met is incredible. the way she describes the explosion - you can feel the dust in your throat. reminds me of how delillo writes about disaster

Theo's relationship with his mom is so tender. the painting becomes a stand-in for her.

"And isn't the whole point of things - beautiful things - that they connect you to some larger beauty?"

boris is one of the best characters ive read in years. chaotic and loyal at the same time

got to part 3 and the vegas section drags a bit. still good but could have been trimmed

favorite quote: "that life - whatever else it is - is short. that fate is cruel but maybe not random."

need to read The Secret History next - same author
