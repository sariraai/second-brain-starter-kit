# Reflection: Burnout and Boundaries

feeling really burned out this week. three back to back sprints with no break. the q2 planning meeting was exhausting - everyone has opinions but nobody wants to own anything.

i think i need to set better boundaries at work. saying yes to everything is killing me. that atomic habits thing about identity is relevant here - i keep identifying as "the reliable one" which means i never say no.

also haven't been to the gym since tuesday. haven't been sleeping well. it all compounds.

things that help when i feel like this:
- long walk with no podcast, just silence
- cooking something from scratch (maybe make mom's pasta sauce this weekend?)
- calling someone instead of texting
- writing like this, honestly

i should probably talk to someone professional about this. its been a pattern for years. not crisis level but a low hum of exhaustion that never fully goes away.
