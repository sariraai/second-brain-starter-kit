# Atomic Habits — James Clear

- habits are the compound interest of self improvement
- 1% better every day = 37x better in a year
- forget about goals, focus on systems
- "you do not rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your systems"
- the 4 laws: make it obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying
- habit stacking - pair a new habit with an existing one ("after I pour my morning coffee, I will meditate for one minute")
- environment design > willpower
- pointing and calling - say out loud what you're about to do
