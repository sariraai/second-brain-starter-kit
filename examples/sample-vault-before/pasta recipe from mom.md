Mom's Sunday Pasta Sauce

San marzano tomatoes (2 cans, 28oz each) - crush by hand!! do NOT use blender
olive oil (generous)
6 cloves garlic, sliced thin
1 onion diced
fresh basil - add at the end not the beginning
red pepper flakes
salt
sugar (just a pinch if the tomatoes are acidic)

optional: pork neck bones or italian sausage browned first

cook LOW AND SLOW. minimum 3 hours. 4 is better.

she said the secret is letting the garlic get golden not brown. if its brown start over.

serve over rigatoni not spaghetti - the sauce gets inside the tubes

dad always adds parm at the table. mom says pecorino is better.
