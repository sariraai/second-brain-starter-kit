# Welcome to Your Second Brain

You just downloaded a starter kit for building a personal knowledge graph — a connected web of your notes, ideas, and thinking that grows smarter over time.

## What You Need

1. **Claude Code** — the AI assistant that will help you set up and maintain your second brain. You're probably already using it if you're reading this. If not, get it at [claude.ai/code](https://claude.ai/code).

2. **Obsidian** — a free note-taking app. Download it at [obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md). It's free for personal use.

That's it. No coding. No terminal commands. No technical knowledge required.

## How to Start

Open Claude Code in this folder and say:

> **"Run /setup"**

Claude Code will walk you through everything from there — installing Obsidian, setting up your vault, and importing any notes you already have.

## What Happens Next

Once you're set up, your daily workflow is simple:

1. **Capture** — Throughout the day, drop notes, ideas, thoughts, and highlights into your Inbox folder. Don't organize anything — just capture.

2. **Process** — Once a day (or whenever you want), say `/process` to Claude Code. It reads your inbox, adds structure, and creates connections between your notes automatically.

3. **Review** — Say `/morning` to start your day or `/digest` to end it. Say `/weekly` on Fridays for a weekly review of what you captured and connected.

That's the whole system. Capture, process, review. Claude Code handles the organizing.

## Your Notes Stay Private

Everything is stored as plain text files on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud. Your notes are yours.

## Questions?

Just ask Claude Code. It knows this system inside and out. Say "help" or "what can you do?" and it will guide you.
